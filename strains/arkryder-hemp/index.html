THE USEFUL PART

Keep these in your head.

  • UW–Madison Extension's 2024 Cultivar Check Program report lists ArkRyder from High Grade Hemp Seed as CBD-dominant Type III material evaluated across a Midwest grower network. That is a documented trial identity, not authentication of every product using the name.
  • The report publishes ArkRyder averages at three, five, and seven weeks after flowering with small, unequal sample counts. The numbers are useful only with their week, analyte, units, sample count, variability, and multi-site method attached.
  • AOSCA's hemp index separately lists ArkRyder as recommended for inclusion in certification. AOSCA also states that applicant-supplied descriptions and claims remain applicant data; the listing is not a chemistry, performance, retail-authenticity, or compliance guarantee.
  • No reviewed primary record supports a universal aroma, terpene panel, effect, medical outcome, or consumer dose. This field note does not manufacture those claims.
  • For a finished hemp or CBD product, verify the responsible business, exact form, package, lot, dates, ingredients, laboratory report, recall status, and current jurisdictional rule. The cultivar name cannot substitute for the product record.

Small rows deserve small conclusions

Five or six samples at a time point can describe those submitted composites. They cannot establish a permanent cultivar specification, every plant's result, another region's performance, or the status of a consumer package.

Run the field-to-product transfer test

  1. Identity.

    Does the evidence name ArkRyder, the seed provider, seed lot, propagation method, and any certification class?

  2. Site and season.

    Which state, latitude, hardiness zone, year, weather, production system, and management record apply?

  3. Sampling.

    Which plants, plant part, flowering week, composite method, sample count, and exclusions produced the row?

  4. Analysis.

    Which laboratory, method, analytes, acid-to-neutral formula, units, standard deviation, and measurement uncertainty apply?

  5. Rule.

    Which current production program, sampling window, official test, and disposition process governs the actual crop?

  6. Finished product.

    Which responsible business, form, ingredients, package, lot, dates, report, recall check, and consumer-product rule apply?

If a layer changes, the older number becomes background—not a prediction.

Understand what the AOSCA listing does—and does not—say

AOSCA's public Hemp Variety Review Board index lists ArkRyder with High Grade Hemp Seed and documents HP2103 and HP2204, with a 2022 recommendation date. The index explains that the board recommended listed varieties for inclusion in certification and that seed may be certified when production meets the relevant seed-certifying agency's standards.

The same page draws an important boundary: variety descriptions, claims, and supporting research are supplied by applicants, and AOSCA takes no position beyond its review recommendation on the truth of every description or claim. Therefore the index can support the name, applicant, document references, and review status. It cannot support an invented parentage, cannabinoid percentage, aroma, effect, yield, current availability, or finished-lot identity.

Use the name as the first field—not the decision

  • The seller and responsible business are verified through the current official route where a regulated program applies.
  • The exact product form is named: seed, living plant, flower, extract, oil, edible, topical, or another finished item.
  • The package records identity, net quantity, ingredients, warnings, lot or batch, production or package dates, and expiration or best-by language where required.
  • The laboratory report matches the same product and lot, uses compatible units, names the sample matrix and methods, and shows complete panels and status.
  • The current recall and regulator check uses the exact product and identifier.
  • No aroma, terpene, effect, treatment, safety, “THC-free,” drug-test, or legal claim is inferred from ArkRyder alone.
