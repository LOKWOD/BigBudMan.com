NBS CBD-1:
the record before the claim.

A public variety certificate and federal germplasm accession make this CBD-hemp name more traceable than most. They still do not turn a cultivar identity into a finished-product chemistry, effect, legal status, or medical promise.

THE USEFUL PART

Keep these in your head.

  • USDA Plant Variety Protection record 201900403 identifies the denomination NBS CBD-1, experimental name 17-2-6-17, applicant Phylos Bioscience, filing date September 5, 2019, and certificate issue date June 22, 2020.
  • The application says the selected family was increased from 50 plants in July 2018 and chosen for uniformity and CBD content. It also says the exact source cross is unknown because the earlier population contained more than 20 publicly available day-neutral types that were randomly intermated.
  • The applicant describes the variety as day-neutral, feminized-seed material with approximate maturity 80 days after emergence and several morphological traits. These are applicant-supplied variety descriptions—not independent performance, chemistry, or consumer-effect findings.
  • USDA's germplasm database lists NBS CBD-1 as accession PI 699239, received as seed in October 2021, with the same PVP and experimental identifiers. The record currently says the accession is unavailable and has no images.
  • A name, PVP certificate, or accession cannot authenticate a retail package. Verify the responsible business, exact form, lot, dates, ingredients, matched laboratory report, recall status, and current production and finished-product rules.

Read applicant-described traits as claims with scope

The PVP application describes NBS CBD-1 as day-neutral and says flowering begins without a changing photoperiod. It reports approximate maturity 80 days after emergence and describes feminized seed, pink pistils, broad leaves, and strong branching. Those statements are useful for distinguishing the filed variety record; they are not a guarantee for an unknown seed lot, environment, production method, plant, or harvest.

The application also records measurements in defined conditions, including potted plants, and leaves many disease-response fields untested. Do not fill an untested box with a seller claim, infer resistance from silence, or copy a single morphological measurement into a universal buying promise.

PVP

Protection is not product verification

A Plant Variety Protection record documents a variety-ownership claim and filed description. It is not a consumer laboratory report, dispensary license, seed germination test, FDA approval, or certificate that a current product meets a jurisdiction's hemp threshold.
