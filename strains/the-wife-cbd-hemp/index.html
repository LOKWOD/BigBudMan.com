THE USEFUL PART

Keep these in your head.

  • Utah State University identifies The Wife by name in outdoor floral-hemp research near Logan, Utah. The Extension synthesis says it appeared in the broader 2021 cultivar trial and in a separate 2021 cultivar-plus-irrigation experiment.
  • The cited Utah work describes The Wife as late relative to the other cultivars in that study. That is a regional research classification—not a universal flowering calendar or a promise about another source, latitude, season, or production system.
  • USU reports useful 2021 measurements for the studied material, including cultivar-trial biomass, CBD yield, and total-THC-at-harvest values. Those results belong to that trial and cannot be copied onto a retail label or an unknown lot.
  • The research also shows why timing matters: cannabinoids in the irrigation experiment peaked before the selected harvest, so a name and a maturity label did not replace repeated field observation and testing.
  • No breeder, lineage, terpene profile, aroma, effect, medical outcome, price, rating, current availability, or verified cultivar photograph is supplied here because the cited primary records do not establish those claims for a current product.

Keep the two 2021 comparisons separate

Evidence laneWhat USU reportsWhat cannot transfer
Broader cultivar trialThe Wife was among the named cultivars compared in 2021 for flowering, biomass, cannabinoid concentration, and cannabinoid yieldA universal ranking, current seed identity, or another region's expected result
Cultivar + irrigation trialThe Wife replaced Trump in the 2021 three-cultivar irrigation comparison with Abacus and Tokyo PlusA conclusion that irrigation system or rate will have the same effect in another field
Regional settingHand-transplanted outdoor material, 5-by-5-foot spacing, near Logan, with the trial's own season and managementGreenhouse, indoor, container, dry-farm, different latitude, or consumer-package performance
Published synthesisUSU Extension connects the field work to peer-reviewed 2025 papers and states the decision lessons in October 2026A breeder certificate, seed-lot certification, product approval, or clinical evidence

Do not merge the two experiments into one imaginary result. The cultivar-only comparison and the cultivar-plus-irrigation experiment asked different questions. A number from one table cannot silently become the answer to the other.

These measurements are valuable because they are attached to a design, site, year, sample, units, and analysis. They are not label copy. CBD yield per acre is not CBD percentage, and a field compliance sample is not a finished-product contaminant panel or consumer certificate of analysis.

Build the identity chain before borrowing the study

  1. Name the research object.

    The paper and Extension synthesis identify a cultivar called The Wife in 2021 Utah trials. Preserve that exact scope.

  2. Name the material in hand.

    Seed, clone, harvested flower, extract, ingredient, and packaged product are different objects. Record the responsible business, source, lot, and dates.

  3. Ask for the connection.

    A seller should be able to explain how the current lot is connected to the named cultivar. The name alone is not chain of custody or genetic authentication.

  4. Match the laboratory report.

    Verify the exact product and lot, matrix, laboratory, sample and report dates, methods, analytes, units, limits, qualifiers, and report status.

  5. Check the current rule.

    Production hemp, processed ingredients, retail flower, food, supplements, cosmetics, medicines, possession, shipping, and travel can sit under different federal, state, tribal, and local rules.

  6. Keep the decision bounded.

    Reject the product—or buy nothing—when identity, lot evidence, condition, claims, or current legality cannot be reconciled.
