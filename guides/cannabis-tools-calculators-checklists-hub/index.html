Editorial navigation and recordkeeping tools for adults 21+. They do not provide medical or legal advice, personal dosing, product certification, laboratory testing, emergency diagnosis, or permission to use cannabis. For urgent symptoms call 911; for a suspected U.S. poisoning or exposure call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222 or use Poison.org.

THE USEFUL PART

Keep these in your head.

  • Choose by decision: label math, price comparison, lot verification, claim checking, product response, shopping, storage, emergency planning, or research reading.
  • Enter only what the package, receipt, official notice, study, or observed event actually supports. Leave unknowns blank instead of estimating them into facts.
  • The interactive tools run in the browser and are not a substitute for preserving the original package, lot, certificate, receipt, warning, or professional record.
  • Print or copy a result only after adding the source date, product identity, and unresolved limitations. A clean worksheet can still be wrong if the input is wrong.
  • Stop using a tool when the decision becomes medical, legal, emergency, laboratory, electrical, or product-specific beyond the page's stated boundary.

Start by photographing or transcribing the label. Record units exactly and keep package total separate from serving amount. A record is a handoff, not a verdict. Separate what was observed from what is inferred. Do not re-expose someone to prove causation. Severe or worsening symptoms, trouble breathing, unresponsiveness, seizure, collapse, chest pain, dangerous behavior, or another emergency belong with 911 rather than a worksheet.

Use a no-buy and no-use outcome. A tool should make missing evidence visible, not pressure the reader to complete a purchase. The repository has no approved affiliate identifier, so these tools do not rank merchants or earn from the products being compared.

Use the five-step tool workflow

  1. Name one decision.

    Do not open a calculator when the real question is whether the product, seller, warning, or medical situation is trustworthy.

  2. Preserve the source object.

    Keep the package, lot, receipt, official notice, certificate, study, device manual, or observed event beside the worksheet.

  3. Enter facts and unknowns separately.

    Use the stated unit and date. Label an estimate as an estimate. Never let a blank field silently become zero.

  4. Read the boundary before the output.

    A result may check arithmetic or organize evidence while remaining unable to establish safety, legality, identity, causation, compatibility, or personal response.

  5. Choose the correct handoff.

    Save, print, or copy the record for the seller, responsible company, regulator, clinician, Poison Control, emergency service, landlord, or household plan that actually owns the next decision.

All interactive calculations occur in the browser. Big Bud Man does not ask readers to create an account for these tools. Avoid entering information you would not want stored in the browser, printout, screenshot, clipboard, or shared device.
