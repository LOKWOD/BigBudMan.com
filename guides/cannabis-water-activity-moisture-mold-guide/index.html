THE USEFUL PART

Keep these in your head.

  • Water activity describes how available water is under defined equilibrium and temperature conditions. Moisture content describes how much water is present. They are related, but one is not a universal conversion of the other.
  • A room or jar relative-humidity reading is not automatically the tested flower's water-activity result. Device accuracy, temperature, equilibration, placement, headspace, package opening, and sample preparation all matter.
  • A passing result belongs to the sampled batch and named method at the reported time. It does not prove that every unit stayed sealed, dry, uncontaminated, correctly stored, or unchanged after testing.
  • Jurisdiction limits are legal quality-control rules, not a national freshness score. Washington, for example, applies a specific water-activity failure threshold to usable cannabis; other programs can use different panels, methods, categories, or limits.
  • Quarantine flower with visible growth, dampness, condensation, damaged seals, unexpected odor, water exposure, recall status, or a lot mismatch. Do not inhale or taste it to investigate.

Keep four different measurements in separate lanes

MeasurementWhat it describesWhat it cannot establish alone
Water activity (aᴡ)Available water measured under a specified equilibrium, temperature, instrument and methodTotal water, microbial count, toxin absence, package integrity, freshness or safety
Moisture contentThe amount or proportion of water in the tested material under the stated methodHow freely that water is available, a universal aᴡ conversion, or condition of every retail unit
Relative humidityWater vapor in the surrounding air relative to saturation at that temperatureA laboratory result for flower merely because the sensor sits in a room, tote or consumer jar
Microbial or mycotoxin resultThe organisms, counts, targets or toxins covered by the named panel and methodEvery possible organism, unsampled material, later storage history or a general mold-free certificate

The FDA's general water-activity technical guide explains the core relationship between water activity and equilibrium relative humidity. That food-science definition helps decode the term; it does not turn a food threshold into a cannabis rule or make a consumer sensor a certified cannabis laboratory instrument.

Match the result to the exact evidence chain

  1. Start at the package.

    Record the responsible business, product identity, form, net quantity, lot or batch, package date, expiration or use-by date where present, seal condition, and the jurisdiction.

  2. Open the correct report route.

    Use the regulator, licensed seller, responsible business, or package-linked certificate route. A screenshot, copied PDF, QR destination, or similarly named strain is not enough.

  3. Match the identifiers.

    Require the same lot or batch, laboratory, report number, sample date, product category, and status. Note whether water activity, moisture, microbial, and mycotoxin results are actually present.

  4. Read the method and units.

    Record the reported value, units, limit, method, sample type, qualifiers, and any pass/fail statement. Do not silently convert a percent, decimal, relative-humidity reading, or jurisdiction limit.

  5. Preserve the time boundary.

    The test describes the submitted sample at a point in the chain. Compare later package damage, storage excursion, condensation, opening history, and recall information separately.

Use a state rule as a rule, not a universal quality score

Evidence layerWashington exampleConsumer boundary
Required fieldWAC 314-55-102 lists water activity among required cannabis quality-control fieldsA different jurisdiction may require a different panel or classify the product differently
Usable cannabis limitThe rule states that usable cannabis fails water-activity testing above 0.65 aᴡDo not copy that number onto another state, hemp product, edible, home grow, device, or unverified lot
Solid edible limitThe same rule gives a different threshold for solid edible productsProduct category is part of the rule; one threshold does not cover every cannabis form
Laboratory chainThe rule sits inside certified laboratory quality-control requirementsA home reading can help notice conditions but cannot be relabeled as the required certified result

Oregon likewise directs regulated marijuana items through its current testing rules and accredited, licensed laboratories. These examples show why the jurisdiction, product category, laboratory status, method, and current rule date belong beside the number.

Use a home humidity reading as a condition clue

DEVICEExact model · range · stated accuracy · battery · calibration/check history
SETUPClean dry container · sensor placement · headspace · no direct contact unless maker allows
CONDITIONSTemperature · time sealed · recent opening · added humidity product · room transition
PACKAGEOriginal label · lot · seal · dates · storage instruction · access history
OBSERVATIONCondensation · damp clumping · visible growth · unusual odor · leak or water exposure
DECISIONContinue locked storage · quarantine · seller/maker/regulator contact · disposal route

A consumer hygrometer may help document a changing storage environment. It does not replace the original package, a lot-matched report, a regulator-required method, or a microbial panel. Avoid repeatedly opening a suspect package to chase a preferred reading.
