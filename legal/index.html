HOW TO READ THE MAP

Legal is not one switch.

Adult-use legal

Adults 21+ have legal possession under state law; limits, public-use rules, local rules, and retail availability still vary.

Medical only

Only qualified, registered patients receive state-law protections; adult-use possession and sales remain illegal.

Limited CBD / defense

The state has a narrow low-THC/CBD law or legal defense, not a comprehensive medical or adult-use program.

No public program

The state has no public medical or adult-use cannabis program.
