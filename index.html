NEW YORK LEGAL BASICS

Know the edges.

Adults 21+ may possess up to three ounces of cannabis and 24 grams of concentrate under New York law. That does not legalize unlicensed sales, vehicle use, impaired driving, state-line travel, or federal-property possession.

THE SIX NON-NEGOTIABLES

A responsible-use baseline

  1. 01

    Adults only.
    No access, use, or youth-targeted packaging.

  2. 02

    No impaired driving.
    Cars, boats, machinery, and ladders can wait.

  3. 03

    Wait for the format.
    Delayed products do not care that you are impatient.

  4. 04

    Keep the package.
    Identity and emergency information matter.

  5. 05

    Lock every product.
    Out of sight is not secure.

  6. 06

    Notice the pattern.
    Use should not quietly shrink the rest of life.
