About the publication

A weed site
with a bullshit filter.

Who publishes this site, what the editorial team actually does, where the evidence comes from, and how readers can challenge a claim.

Legal cannabis created a strange information problem. Packaging looks scientific, menus look precise, strain stories sound ancient, and marketing claims move faster than evidence. New and returning adults are expected to decode all of it at the counter.

What we publish

Big Bud Man covers cannabis basics, product labels, formats, storage, responsible use, famous strain names, practical accessories, and plain-English legal starting points for all 50 states. We do not sell cannabis, arrange sales, publish unverified dispensary promotions, or provide personalized medical advice.

Evidence before vibe

Official sources, current labels, batch information, and transparent uncertainty beat folklore.

Adult without juvenile

Cannabis can be discussed with humor without targeting children or turning intoxication into a stunt.

Useful before exhaustive

The first paragraph should help. The rest should reward the reader who needs depth.

Safety without scolding

Driving, storage, accidental exposure, health cautions, and problematic use belong in the main story.

What “Big Bud Man” means

The name is intentionally a little oversized. The editorial voice is not. The goal is a memorable adult brand that can say, plainly, when a huge THC number or legendary strain story is doing more marketing than informing.

Where we are based

The publication is built in Central New York and gives extra depth to New York’s regulated adult-use market. Nationwide legal information is dated, linked to official sources, and written as education—not legal advice.

Ownership and responsibility

Big Bud Man is an independently operated cannabis-education publication based in Central New York. The Big Bud Man Editorial Team is the named publisher and is responsible for source selection, page scope, writing, review, corrections, and the final publication decision. The site is not a dispensary, laboratory, medical practice, law firm, testing authority, regulator, or product-certification service.

How a page is made

  1. Define one reader decision.

    Each page begins with the question it can answer and the claims it must not make.

  2. Inventory the site.

    Titles, slugs, core intent, recent additions, and related pages are checked before a new URL is approved.

  3. Use a source hierarchy.

    Law begins with statutes, regulators, courts, and official guidance. Health and science begin with government health agencies, systematic reviews, clinical guidance, standards bodies, and original research. Brand and retailer material can document a product claim but cannot validate it.

  4. Write the limits beside the fact.

    Sample, method, population, jurisdiction, date, product, route, uncertainty, and evidence gaps stay attached to the conclusion.

  5. Connect the page.

    New work must have a useful collection path and contextual links in both directions; isolated pages and cosmetic keyword variants are rejected.

  6. Run publication checks.

    The build checks metadata, structured data, internal links and fragments, accessibility rules, sitemap and search coverage, page identity, source-archive integrity, and the dated publication manifest before deployment.

Testing and firsthand-experience boundary

Unless a page explicitly names a documented test, Big Bud Man has not purchased, handled, used, measured, laboratory-tested, clinically evaluated, or personally experienced the product or method discussed. We do not turn manufacturer specifications, consumer anecdotes, generated examples, or another publication's test into our own firsthand claim. Buying guides are evidence and decision frameworks—not disguised reviews.

Commercial separation

The repository currently contains no approved affiliate tracking ID or active affiliate program. When commercial links are introduced, the relationship must be disclosed near the link, qualifying links must use sponsored and nofollow markup, and payment cannot determine coverage, ranking, conclusion, or correction. No cannabis sale, unverified discount, scarcity claim, or generic product insertion is allowed.

Updates, corrections, and dates

A visible reviewed date means the page received a material source, accuracy, usability, or publication review on that date; it does not guarantee that every external rule or product remains unchanged. Significant factual corrections are made in the page and, when useful to readers, explained in the publication update surfaces. To challenge a statement, send the page URL, exact passage, reason, and strongest primary source through the contact form. A correction does not require agreement with the page's tone, but it does require evidence.

Automation and human accountability

Automation assists with research organization, drafts, consistency, link checks, structured data, builds, and deployment monitoring. It does not supply missing firsthand experience, professional licensure, laboratory competence, personal interviews, or legal authority. Published claims remain the responsibility of the editorial team named on the page. The complete editorial policy documents the current rules and automated gates.
